# My Dance Record Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 May 2026

My Dance Record helps dancers and families record dance events, results, notes, photos, backups, optional social images, and optional calendar entries. The app is designed to keep your dance history under your control.

## 1. Information We Process

My Dance Record may process the following information on your device:

- Competition or event names, dates, locations, and multi-day event details.
- Dancer profile names and profile status.
- Dance names, dance numbers, placings, scores, adjudicator names, adjudicator scores, and notes.
- Photos you choose to add to events.
- Backup, export, restore, purchase-entitlement, review-prompt, onboarding, calendar preference, and app-setting information.
- Calendar permission status when you choose to use Apple Calendar entries.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the app's core functionality: recording dance events, keeping dancer histories separate, showing results, searching your dance history, exporting PDFs and backups, creating social images when requested, restoring purchases, and adding or updating calendar entries when enabled.

## 3. Local Storage

My Dance Record is designed to store your dance records locally on your iPhone or Android device in the app's private storage. My Dance Record does not operate its own cloud database or upload your dance records, photos, backups, or results to My Dance Record servers.

## 4. Photos, Files, Backups, and Exports

Photos, exported PDFs, backup files, and social images remain on your device unless you choose to save, export, or share them. Any sharing is started by you using the device's standard sharing features, Photos, Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, cloud storage, or another destination you select.

## 5. Calendar Entries

If you enable calendar entries, the app may add, update, or delete event details such as event name, dancer name, date, and location in the calendar you choose. Calendar access can be changed at any time in your device settings.

## 6. Purchases

The app may use Apple App Store or Google Play purchase systems to unlock paid features and restore previous purchases. Purchase processing is handled by Apple or Google. My Dance Record does not receive your full payment-card details.

## 7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

My Dance Record does not use third-party analytics, advertising, targeted advertising, or tracking technologies.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

You control the information stored in the app. You can edit records, archive dancer profiles, export backups, reset app data, or uninstall the app to remove local app data, subject to normal device backup and device-management behaviour.

## 9. Children's Privacy

My Dance Record can be used by families to record dance information for dancers of different ages, but it is intended to be managed by a parent, guardian, dancer, or responsible adult. The app does not knowingly collect personal information on My Dance Record servers.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. When it changes, the effective date above will be updated.

## 11. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact app.my.inventory@gmail.com.
